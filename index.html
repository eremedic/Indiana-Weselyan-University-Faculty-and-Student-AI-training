What guides this training

Verify

Check sources, citations, calculations and claims. People remain responsible for what they submit.

Protect

Only approved systems may receive protected educational, personal, research or proprietary information.

Show the work

Never fabricate evidence, impersonate someone, or let AI replace the reasoning an assignment tests.

Keep human decisions

Grades, standing and misconduct findings are human decisions. A detector score alone never proves a violation.

How it works

1 · Learn

2 · Practice
